This week's accomplishments were the blocking of a few scene placements with some basic Unity geometry and the beginnings of a ride state machine.  I have mapped out my ride states and am working on features that will highlight the use of them.  My goal here is to show the state of the boat while in motion, as it travels along the ride path.  The ride states that I'm working with currently are:

- idle
- boarding
- on ride
- scene 1
- scene 2
- scene 3
- returning
- unloading
- ride over

A ride state program serves as a framework for various triggers that might occur along the ride path and would be essential to functions like scene detection, lighting and feature useage, and emergency stops if something were to go wrong.
