### Weekly Accomplishments

This week was somewhat slow for me.  I spent some time working through the [Unity 3D Essentials Tutorial](https://learn.unity.com/pathway/unity-essentials/unit/3d-essentials?version=6.0) in preparation of blocking out the scenes for the ride.  I now have a stronger grasp on navigation within the 3D space of my project and feel more comfortable viewing objects from different angles to ensure that everything is spaced correctly. I realized that I would need a foundation to build the scenes on and added ground below the ride path.  Navigating the 3D arena, building, and placing new game objects were this week's primary accomplishments.  Adding walls may be beneficial, as they can be used to create depth within some of the scenes. 

### This Week's Goals

3. Walls for the ride 'building'.
